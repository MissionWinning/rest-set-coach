**1. The idea.** "What would you like to make for glasses?"
Suggested: Between sets at the gym I use my phone to check rest, see what's next and log the set. I want that on glasses: my next set sits beside the rack, I pinch once to log the set, and when rest ends it calls the next set like a rally co-driver calling a turn. This is my real product, Mission Winning ("Log a set. No account. No wearable.").

**2. The spatial moment.** "Picture the moment your Lens is at its best. What are you doing, and what do you see? Why would it be worse on a phone?"
Suggested: I've just racked the bar. A small card stands beside the rack at chest height. Rest hits zero, the card turns green and calls "GO — Set 3 · 5 × 100 · hold depth". I do the set and pinch SET DONE, which logs it, fills a dot and starts the next rest. A phone is worse because my hands are chalky, the phone is on the floor, and unlocking it breaks focus and rest timing. It is NOT a form coach (Fitlens) and NOT body tracking or a ghost race (HYROX Pulse).

**3. Product or overlay.** "Is the virtual thing the product, or an overlay on something real? What stands in for anything the preview can't sense?"
Suggested: The coach card is the product. Nothing real needs to be recognized. Stand-ins: a virtual squat rack (box frame about 120 cm wide × 200 cm tall, 1.5 m in front of start) and a hard-coded plan (Back Squat, 4 × 5 @ 100 kg, 10 s demo rest).

**4. Where things live.** "Where does each thing sit, and where are you looking while you use it? How big is it?"
Suggested: I'm looking at the rack. The card is to the right of the rack, about 140 cm high and about 1.2 m from me, about 40 × 30 cm, facing me. On it: big rest digits (about 8 cm), the next-set line under them (about 3 cm), the GO pace note replacing the digits at zero, set dots along the bottom, and a SET DONE button at least 12 cm wide.

**5. Hands.** "How do you act on it with your hands?"
Suggested: One pinch on the big SET DONE button. No voice, no custom gestures.

**6. When it shows.** "When should each thing appear, and when should it get out of the way? How do you get pointed to it if it's out of view?"
Suggested: During rest the countdown is white/cyan with a small "DEMO 10 s" label. At 3 s left it pulses "READY". At 0 a chime plays from the card, the card turns green and shows the pace-note call (this has to work even without sound). After set 4 it shows "Workout logged ✓ 4/4" and the timer hides. If the card is out of view, the chime is the cue. No dark panels: light text on a tinted, semi-transparent card.

**7. Done.** "How will you know it works? Name 3–5 things you can do or see in the preview."
Suggested:
1. On start, the card appears beside a virtual rack about 1.2 m away and is readable.
2. Pinching SET DONE advances Set 1 → Set 2 and fills one dot.
3. A 10 s rest countdown starts and visibly counts down.
4. At 0 the card turns green and shows "GO — <next set pace note>" (the hero shot, in the first 10 s of the video).
5. After the 4th set it shows "Workout logged 4/4".

**People and consent.** (Asked only if other people are involved.) Suggested: none. It's my own space only; no recording, identifying or following anyone.

**Sign-off.** After plan-lens writes devpost/design-brief.md, it asks whether anything should change. You can approve with "looks good".

Out of scope (already decided in the draft): rep counting, camera form checks, form animations, body tracking, saved history, app sync, multiple exercises, live GPS.
